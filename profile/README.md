# TECHSPACE

> **Build. Explore. Innovate.**

TechSpace is a student-led technical community at **SRM University Delhi-NCR, Sonipat**, focused on building, experimenting, and creating meaningful technology together.

We bring students together through **projects, workshops, hackathons, technical events, open-source initiatives, and collaborative learning.**

---

## ◉ What We Do

**BUILD**
Real-world projects, experiments, and developer-focused initiatives.

**LEARN**
Technical workshops, bootcamps, challenges, and peer-to-peer learning.

**COMPETE**
Hackathons, coding challenges, and innovation-driven events.

**COLLABORATE**
Work with students, mentors, developers, and communities to turn ideas into working products.

---

## 🚀 Our Ecosystem

### BuildLab

A project-based learning environment where students go from **idea → PRD → development → collaboration → deployment**.

Participants work with mentors, GitHub, Discord, and real development workflows to build projects that actually ship.

### Workshops

Hands-on technical sessions covering technologies ranging from **programming and web development to AI/ML, mobile development, and emerging technologies.**

### Events

Hackathons, technical competitions, community sessions, and collaborative events designed to turn learning into experience.

### Open Source

We encourage students to learn modern development practices through **Git, GitHub, pull requests, issues, documentation, and collaborative development.**
